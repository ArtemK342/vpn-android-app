Service description

fsociety VPN is a commercial VPN service based on the AmneziaWG, VLESS+Reality, Trojan, Hysteria2 and OpenVPN+Cloak protocols. The Service encrypts internet traffic and replaces the user's real IP address with one of our server addresses.

The Service is intended exclusively for protecting users' privacy and security online.

We maintain infrastructure across multiple jurisdictions. The list of available servers and countries is subject to change — we continuously work to expand it.

The app may also offer "RF mode" based on the open-source ByeDPI component. It runs on your device without our servers and gets around blocking, but it does not encrypt traffic or hide your IP address.
