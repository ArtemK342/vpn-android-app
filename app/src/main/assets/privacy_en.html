Privacy Policy.

Last updated: April 25, 2026 · Version 1.1

// 01

General

This Privacy Policy describes what data fsociety VPN collects and stores, and how that data is used and protected.

By using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

// 02

Zero-Log Policy

We do not store or collect:

  • Your internet activity history
  • User IP addresses during VPN sessions
  • Session time and duration
  • DNS query data
  • Traffic volume or content
  • Private encryption keys
  • Connection metadata
We are technically unable to provide data about your activity to third parties — because we do not collect it.
// 03

Email Accounts

For email accounts we store only:

  • Email address
  • Password hash (not the password itself)
  • Subscription status
  • Public WireGuard key

The private key is generated on the fly on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

All data is stored in Switzerland.

// 04

Anonymous Accounts

For anonymous accounts (login via 16-digit code) we store no email or password. Only stored:

  • Access code hash
  • Subscription status
  • Public VPN key
This is the maximum possible level of privacy within a functioning service. We cannot identify you as a person.

When creating an anonymous account, the IP address is recorded solely for rate limiting purposes and is not linked to your VPN activity.

// 05

Payment Data

Payments are processed through Telegram Stars. We do not receive or store bank card data. Payment information is processed by Telegram in accordance with their privacy policy.

// 06

Support Data

When you contact support, we store ticket correspondence to resolve your issue. After closing the ticket and deleting the account, all data is deleted.

// 07

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, transfer, or disclose your personal data to third parties.

Since we do not store activity logs — we cannot provide them to authorities or on any other grounds.

// 08

Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time through settings. Upon deletion, all associated data is immediately removed from our servers.

// 09

Contact

For questions related to this Policy, contact us via the in-app support form or Telegram. We respond within 24 hours.
