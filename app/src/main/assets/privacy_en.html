Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Version 1.2

// 01

General

This Policy describes what data the fsociety VPN service (website, API, VPN servers and apps) collects, how long it keeps it and why.

By using the Service, you agree to this Policy. The same text is published on the website and in the app.

// 02

What we don't collect

On our VPN servers (except servers marked ● LOGS ON, see section 4) we do not store:

  • Your browsing history or the addresses you connect to through the VPN
  • DNS queries
  • Traffic content
  • The IP address you connect to the VPN from
  • The exact time and duration of your connections
We have no data about what you do online through the VPN — so we cannot hand it over to anyone.
// 03

What we store and for how long

To run the service, our database (Switzerland) stores:

  • Email account: email, password hash (bcrypt, never the password itself), subscription status, sign-up date — until the account is deleted.
  • Anonymous account: the 16-digit access code, subscription status, sign-up date. No email, name or phone number.
  • Last activity date (sign-in or connection, day precision) — to delete inactive anonymous accounts and count active users.
  • IP hash on anonymous sign-up: the IP itself is not saved, only its HMAC-SHA256 — to limit account creation (1 per day per IP). The hash is deleted after 24 hours.
  • Connection keys: for AmneziaWG — public key, server, internal tunnel address and issue date; for VLESS, Trojan, Hysteria2 and OpenVPN — a personal connection ID. The AmneziaWG private key is generated on our server when the config is issued, sent only to you and never stored. After you disconnect, the key record (with the traffic volume of that session) is deleted within 7 days.
  • Traffic volume: free accounts only — a single number for the current month to apply the limit (1 GB anonymous, 3 GB email accounts). No addresses or content. Deleted when the month ends. Paid subscriptions are not metered.
  • Support tickets: messages are kept for 10 days after the ticket is created and then deleted automatically.

Inactive anonymous accounts are deleted automatically: after 5 days without sign-ins or connections if there was never a subscription, and after 30 days if there was one.

// 04

Servers with logs enabled

Some servers may have logs enabled — for example, while we launch and debug a new protocol. Such servers are marked in the app and on the website with a red ● LOGS ON label.

Logs on these servers may contain the IP address you connect from, your connection ID, timestamps and the addresses you access. They are kept for no more than 30 days and used only for diagnostics and abuse prevention.

If that doesn't suit you, choose servers without the ● LOGS ON label.
// 05

Website, API and email

The website and API run behind Cloudflare. Cloudflare sees your IP address and processes it under its own privacy policy. If Cloudflare is unreachable, the app contacts our API directly.

Our web server keeps no request log (access log): IP addresses and page addresses are not recorded.

Emails (address confirmation, password reset, a copy of a ticket conversation on your request) are sent via Resend — it receives only the recipient address and the email text.

// 06

Support log in the app

The app settings include a support log. It is off by default. If you turn it on, technical events (connection, errors, network changes) are written only to your phone. Keys, passwords, tokens and emails are removed from it.

The log reaches us only if you send it yourself with the Share button. We use it only to solve your issue; a log sent in a ticket is deleted with the ticket after 10 days.

// 07

Protocols and third-party components

The app uses open-source components. They run on your device and send no data to their authors:

  • AmneziaWG — the main protocol
  • sing-box — VLESS+Reality, Trojan, Hysteria2 and split tunneling
  • OpenVPN and Cloak — fallback protocol
  • ByeDPI — "RF mode"

"RF mode" runs entirely on your phone without our servers: traffic goes to websites directly, and ByeDPI only modifies packets to get around blocking. This traffic does not pass through our servers and we don't see it — but your IP address is not hidden from websites in this mode.

// 08

Payments

Payments are made via Telegram Stars. We do not receive or store card details. Payments are processed by Telegram under its own privacy policy.

// 09

Third parties and requests

We do not sell or share your data. Apart from us, data is processed only by the services in sections 5 and 8 (Cloudflare, Resend, Telegram), exactly to the extent described there.

On a request from authorities we can technically provide only what is listed in sections 3 and 4. We have no history of your activity.

// 10

Data deletion

You can delete your account at any time on the website — in your dashboard or on the Delete account page. The account, keys, subscription and tickets are removed from the database immediately. Records on servers marked ● LOGS ON expire on their own (within 30 days).

Questions — via a ticket in the app or your dashboard, or via Telegram. We respond within 24 hours.
