General

This Policy describes what data the fsociety VPN service (website, API, VPN servers and apps) collects, how long it keeps it and why.

By using the Service, you agree to this Policy. The same text is published on the website and in the app.

The Service is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect children's data; an anonymous account contains no personal data at all.

// 02

What we don't collect

On our VPN servers (except servers marked ● LOGS ON, see section 4) we do not store:

  • Your browsing history or the addresses you connect to through the VPN
  • DNS queries
  • Traffic content
  • The IP address you connect to the VPN from
  • The exact time and duration of your connections
We have no data about what you do online through the VPN — so we cannot hand it over to anyone.
// 03

What we store and for how long

To run the service, our database (Switzerland) stores:

  • Email account: email, password hash (bcrypt, never the password itself), subscription status, sign-up date — until the account is deleted.
  • Anonymous account: a hash of the 16-digit access code (HMAC-SHA256 — we don't keep the code itself; only you have it: in the app, your browser or written down), subscription status, sign-up date. No email, name or phone number.
  • Last activity date (sign-in or connection, day precision) — to delete inactive anonymous accounts and count active users.
  • Pricing region (Russia, Europe or the rest of the world) — set from your country at sign-up so prices are shown in the right currency. The country and IP themselves are not stored. You can switch to Russian prices in your dashboard; other changes go through support.
  • IP on anonymous sign-up is not written to the database — neither the address nor its hash. To limit account creation (5 per day per IP) it is kept only in server memory for at most a day and is gone after a restart.
  • Connection keys: for AmneziaWG — public key, server, internal tunnel address and issue date; for VLESS, Trojan, Hysteria2 and OpenVPN — a personal connection ID. In the app (from version 0.0.3.9) the AmneziaWG private key is generated on your phone and never leaves it — the server only receives the public key. If you download a config on the website, the key is generated on our server, sent only to you and never stored. After you disconnect, the key record (with the traffic volume of that session) is deleted within 7 days.
  • Traffic volume: free email accounts only — a single number for the current month to apply the 3 GB limit. No addresses or content. Deleted when the month ends. Paid subscriptions are not metered.
  • Support tickets: messages are kept for 10 days after the ticket is created and then deleted automatically.

Inactive anonymous accounts are deleted automatically: after 5 days without sign-ins or connections if there was never a subscription, and after 30 days if there was one.

Database backups are made daily and stored in Proton Drive (Switzerland), which encrypts files end to end. Each backup is kept for 30 days and then deleted.

// 04

Servers with logs enabled

Some servers may have logs enabled — for example, while we launch and debug a new protocol. Such servers are marked in the app and on the website with a red ● LOGS ON label.

Logs on these servers may contain the IP address you connect from, your connection ID, timestamps and the addresses you access. They are kept for no more than 30 days and used only for diagnostics and abuse prevention.

If that doesn't suit you, choose servers without the ● LOGS ON label.
// 05

Website, API and email

The website runs behind Cloudflare: when you open the website or your dashboard, Cloudflare sees your IP address and the content of the requests and processes them under its own privacy policy. The website is also available at the mirror address fsociety-vpn.xyz — the same website on the same server, also behind Cloudflare.

The app talks to our API directly, bypassing Cloudflare:

  • anonymous account — all requests, including code sign-in and account creation: Cloudflare sees neither your IP, nor the code, nor the keys;
  • email account — VPN config requests (keys and connection passwords); sign-in, tickets and the rest go through Cloudflare.

If the direct address is unreachable (for example, blocked by your ISP), the app temporarily sends these requests through Cloudflare. For maximum privacy, use an anonymous account in the app rather than on the website.

Our web server keeps no request log (access log): IP addresses and page addresses are not recorded.

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads or third-party counters; fonts are served from our server. The country used to show prices in your currency comes from Cloudflare and is not stored. Your browser keeps only sign-in, language and theme — see the Cookie Policy.

Emails (address confirmation and password reset) are sent via Resend — it receives only the recipient address and the email text.

// 06

Support log in the app

The app settings include a support log. It is off by default. If you turn it on, technical events (connection, errors, network changes) are written only to your phone. Keys, passwords, tokens and emails are removed from it.

The log reaches us only if you send it yourself with the Share button. We use it only to solve your issue; a log sent in a ticket is deleted with the ticket after 10 days.

// 07

Protocols and third-party components

The app uses open-source components. They run on your device and send no data to their authors:

  • AmneziaWG — the main protocol
  • sing-box — VLESS+Reality, Trojan, Hysteria2 and split tunneling
  • OpenVPN and Cloak — fallback protocol
  • ByeDPI — "RF mode"
  • zapret — "RF mode" in the Windows app

"RF mode" runs entirely on your device without our servers: traffic goes to websites directly, and ByeDPI or zapret only modify packets to get around blocking. This traffic does not pass through our servers and we don't see it — but your IP address is not hidden from websites in this mode.

// 08

Browser extension

The extension for Chrome and Yandex Browser sends only browser traffic through our HTTPS proxy — other programs on your computer connect directly.

  • On the proxy servers, as on our other servers without the ● LOGS ON label, visited websites, your IP address and connection times are not recorded.
  • To sign in to the proxy, the extension gets a temporary password from our API, valid for 12 hours. The proxy checks its signature itself, without a database lookup.
  • The browser stores only your sign-in, the server list, the temporary password and the extension settings.
  • The extension changes the browser's proxy settings and, with "WebRTC protection" on, the WebRTC setting; it restores them when the VPN is turned off.
  • The extension has no analytics, ads or counters.

Payments

You can pay for a subscription with:

  • Telegram Stars — in our bot @fsocietynet_agent or from the app;
  • Tribute — via Telegram, by card;
  • cryptocurrency via the Heleket service.

We do not receive or store card or crypto wallet details: payments are processed by Telegram, Tribute or Heleket under their own privacy policies.

We keep a payment record: method, amount and currency, date, plan and the payment number at the payment service. For payments via Telegram (Stars, Tribute) — also your numeric Telegram ID, so the bot can deliver the activation code. An activation code (FS-XXXX-XXXX) is stored with its issue and activation dates and the account it was activated in.

Payment records are needed for refunds and accounting. After the account is deleted they are kept for another 30 days — or, if the subscription was still running, 30 days after it ends — and then deleted automatically. Codes bought in the bot and never activated are not tied to an account.

Data deletion

You can delete your account at any time on the website — in your dashboard or on the Delete account page. The account, keys, subscription and tickets are removed from the database immediately; payment records after 30 days, or 30 days after the subscription ends (section 9). Records on servers marked ● LOGS ON expire on their own (within 30 days), and from database backups within 30 days at the latest.
